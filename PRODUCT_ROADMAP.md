# Product Roadmap

## Next Release

### Reporting improvements
Priority: High
Add more detailed sales and customer reports.

### Mobile dashboard redesign
Priority: High
Improve navigation and usability on smaller screens.

### Notification controls
Priority: Medium
Allow users to customize which notifications they receive.

### Faster dashboard loading
Priority: High
Reduce dashboard loading time for accounts with large amounts of data.

### Simplified onboarding
Priority: Medium
Make the first-time setup process easier for new customers.

### Customer activity timeline
Priority: Low
Provide a clearer chronological history of customer interactions.

## Product question
The team needs to decide whether reporting improvements, performance improvements, or mobile improvements should receive the most development capacity in the next release.
