# Bug Reports

## BUG-101 — Dashboard loads slowly
Status: Open
Priority: High
Reported by: Fresh Choice

Description:
The dashboard can take several seconds to load when an account contains a large number of customer records.

Impact:
Users may experience delays when reviewing sales activity.

## BUG-102 — Duplicate notifications
Status: Open
Priority: Medium
Reported by: Blue Market

Description:
Some users occasionally receive the same notification more than once.

Impact:
Users may become annoyed or ignore future notifications.

## BUG-103 — Mobile navigation is difficult
Status: Open
Priority: High
Reported by: Urban Retail

Description:
Several navigation controls are difficult to use on smaller screens.

Impact:
Sales staff using phones may have difficulty reaching important customer information.

## BUG-104 — Report export formatting
Status: Open
Priority: Medium
Reported by: Green Foods

Description:
Some exported reports have inconsistent column widths.

Impact:
Users sometimes need to manually adjust exported files.

## BUG-105 — Onboarding instructions unclear
Status: Open
Priority: Medium
Reported by: Blue Market

Description:
New users are unsure which steps should be completed first during initial setup.

Impact:
Longer onboarding time and additional support requests.
